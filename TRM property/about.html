About TRM Property Group

Our History

TRM Property Group is a luxury real estate agency in Gauteng, South Africa, offering residential sales, commercial leasing and property management services. Established in 2018. Over the last eight years, the organization has evolved into a respected property brand with active branches located in Johannesburg, Pretoria and Midrand. With the agency’s growth, it has relied on traditional marketing, local listings and third-party portals, making a centralized, modern, custom digital presence a critical need.

Mission

To provide buyers, sellers, tenants and investors with seamless, transparent and high-value real estate solutions, supported by professional integrity, technology-driven service and personalized care.

Vision

To be the premier technology integrated real estate organization in Southern Africa, setting an example for client interaction, property accessibility and modern digital property management.

Target Audience

We serve prospective homebuyers,real estate investors, residential tenants, and corporate entities seeking strategic real estate partnerships.
